About

A simple way to find what to cook

MealFinder is a web-based meal discovery tool. Type in a dish or an ingredient, and it searches TheMealDB's open recipe database for matching meals — no account, no clutter, just recipes.

Every result shows a meal ID, its name, a photo, and full cooking instructions, so you can decide what to make and start cooking right away.

  • Search meals instantly
  • View recipe information at a glance
  • Read full cooking instructions
  • Explore meals from different cuisines
